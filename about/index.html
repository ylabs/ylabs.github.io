About

Engineering leadership for AI in the public interest

Ylab is run by David Yoon, an engineering leader who has spent twenty years building teams and software for health systems, governments and nonprofits.

The story

David started as a computer engineer, writing computer-vision code in C for medical manufacturing in Copenhagen, then scaling SparkNotes to around ten million users in New York.

As founding engineer and CTO at Samasource, he built one of the earliest human-in-the-loop data annotation platforms for machine learning, grew engineering to fifteen as the company scaled from five people to five hundred, and helped open its Nairobi office. He went on to lead engineering at FWD.us, the immigration-reform group founded by Mark Zuckerberg and Reid Hoffman.

In 2020 he was on the founding core team of U.S. Digital Response, embedded with St. Louis County during COVID-19 and leading engineering and product for Project N95, a national PPE clearinghouse with around forty engineers that matched 3,700 suppliers to 6,000 requests. He then led engineering at Antara Health in Nairobi, rebuilding the EMR that frontline workers use for hypertension and diabetes care, and was director of engineering at Cityblock Health.

Since 2023 he has been building with AI hands-on, first at South Park Commons, and he now advises UNDP and the African Development Bank on AI across Africa. Ylab is where that work becomes public: open evaluations and tools that help institutions decide whether AI is ready for the people they serve.
