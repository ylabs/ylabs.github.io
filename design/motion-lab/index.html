# Things to play with

Each stage below is interactive: move your cursor over it, drag, or use the controls. Under each one is how the motion works and where it could live on the site.

Like code.storage, none of these use a physics engine. Each is a few lines of spring, gravity or damping math run every frame.

## Case swarm

Every case is a ball Move to push · drag on a phone

How it moves. 1,400 chrome spheres drawn in two batches. Each is pulled toward a home spot by a capped spring, loses 8% of its speed per frame, and is pushed away from the cursor inside a small radius.

On ylab. The hero of the evals page. Balls are test cases; the rust ones failed. "Sort" splits them into a pass pile and a fail pile, which is the whole offer in one gesture.

## Liquid chrome

Hover to agitate · drag to turn

How it moves. A smooth sphere whose surface is pushed in and out by layered sine waves every frame. Hovering eases the wave height up, and dragging spins it with momentum that decays.

On ylab. A signature object for the homepage, standing in for "the institution": calm at rest, unsettled when you poke it. Made entirely in code, so there's no 3D model to commission.

## Hanging captions

Grab a tag and throw it

How it moves. Verlet rope: each tag hangs from a dotted chain of 16 points. Gravity pulls them down, and the chain is re-measured 14 times per frame so it keeps its length.

On ylab. code.storage's black caption tags, made physical. Section labels or client quotes could hang from the top of a section and swing when you scroll past.

## Terminal field

Move to send ripples

How it moves. A grid of characters picked by brightness from " .·:-=+*#%@". Brightness comes from two slow waves, a word mask, and a ring wave spreading from the cursor.

On ylab. A lightweight hero for the README direction. It's plain text on a 2D canvas, so it costs almost nothing and suits the monospace page.

## Restless headline

Building institutions for the AI era.

Run your cursor through the words

How it moves. Every letter is its own spring. The cursor pushes letters away, they tip in the direction they're thrown, and they ease back into place.

On ylab. The homepage headline, so the first thing a visitor touches reacts. The text stays real HTML, so it's still readable and searchable.

## Institution of dots

Built on judgment Move to disturb

How it moves. A portico traced in about 600 dots. "Collapse" switches each dot to gravity with a bouncy floor; "Rebuild" switches back to springs that pull every dot home.

On ylab. The "building institutions" idea, literally. It could collapse as you scroll into a section about what AI disrupts, then rebuild at "how we help".

--- All six respect your system's reduce-motion setting. They pause when scrolled off screen.
