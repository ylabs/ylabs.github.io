## Now offering: AI evaluations

Every vendor arrives with a benchmark. None of them were run on your patients, your caseworkers' files or your staff's actual jobs.

We build evaluations from your own cases, graded by the people who do the work, so you know what a system gets right, where it fails and what it costs before you sign.

**Sample eval sheet**

+-------------+--------------------------------+
| Task        | Discharge summary drafting     |
| Systems     | [Vendor A] [Vendor B] [Base]   |
| Cases       | [n], de-identified, your own   |
| Graded by   | Your clinicians, shared rubric |
| Checks      | Accuracy, omissions, safety,   |
|             | time saved                     |
| Verdict     | [Result goes here]             |
+-------------+--------------------------------+

## How it works

  1. 01  Define. What good looks like here, written with your clinicians, officers or managers.
  2. 02  Test. Candidate systems side by side, on your cases, under your constraints.
  3. 03  Decide. A plain report your board, regulator and staff can all read.
