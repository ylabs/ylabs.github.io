New · AI evaluations for institutions

The demo worked. Will it work on your ward?

Every vendor arrives with a benchmark. None of them were run on your patients, your caseworkers' files or your staff's actual jobs.

We build evaluations from your own cases, graded by the people who do the work, so you know what a system gets right, where it fails, and what it costs before you sign.

  1. 01Define
    What good looks like here, written with your clinicians, officers or managers.
  2. 02Test
    Run candidate systems side by side on your cases, under your constraints.
  3. 03Decide
    A plain report your board, regulator and staff can all read.

Scope an evaluation

Three rooms where AI is decided.

Healthcare

II.

Government

III.

Future of work

For employers, funders and talent networks: what AI changes about roles and teams, and how to build human capability alongside it.
